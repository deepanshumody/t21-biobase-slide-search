What these data can and can't support

Supported by the processed measurements

  • Whether a feature's mean signal is above or below a cutoff.
  • Whether it shows up in negative controls, and how strongly compared with samples.
  • How many sample replicates carry it, which separates recurring signal from single-well events.
  • How each filter did on the cohort A mixture, where membership is known.

Needs more evidence

  • Raw read counts and read quality. Normalized signal hides how few reads a rare feature has.
  • Taxonomy and sequence identity. Feature IDs are hashed, so you can't name the organism here.
  • Plate layout and control type, to tell reagent contamination from sample-to-control leakage.
  • Spike-ins or qPCR to confirm absolute abundance. Whether either filter's accuracy carries over to other cohorts.
Data: MicrobIEM known-mixture benchmark and NASA OSD-890 (see 03-qc/NOTICE.md). This report reviews filter decisions; it doesn't establish biological outcomes.
